Digital Citizenship

What is digital citizenship

Digital citizenship is the responsible, safe, and respectful use of technology—especially the internet, social media, and digital devices.

What is digital law?

Digital law refers to the rules, rights, and legal responsibilities that apply when people use technology, the internet, and digital devices.

What is AUP?

AUP stands for Acceptable Use Policy. It is a set of rules that explains how people are allowed to use computers, networks, internet access, or digital devices in places like schools, businesses, and organizations.

What is plagiarism?

Plagiarism is using someone else’s words, ideas, images, or work and pretending they are your own without giving proper credit.
