Whether hiking scenic sandstone trails or stargazing beneath world-class dark skies, it's the ultimate red-rock escape to recharge and explore. (copy from Gemini)

Backpacking Red Castle

Trek deep into Utah's Uinta Mountains to Red Castle, where dramatic crimson peaks tower over serene alpine lakes and wildflower meadows. It’s the ultimate backcountry backpacking escape for quiet solitude, rugged trails, and unforgettable wilderness camping. (copy from Gemini)

Lodgings

Red Castle is just off the beaten path. On your way in, you'll spend a night at the Best Western in Evanston Wyoming. Leaving early from Evanston will get you a jumpstart on your hiking trail the next morning.

Transportation
