Hiking Red Castle

One early morning in the summer before I started the third grade, I climbed into the cramped backseat of a Toyota truck and drove some hours out of Utah, through Wyoming, and finally dropped into the Uintas on the northern side.

Though my pack weighed just 15 lbs fully loaded, my shoulders were more than grateful for my brother to hike back and snag my pack for the final 100 yards of trail to our first night's camp.
