About Condorcet

The Condorcet winner of an election is the candidate who would win head-to-head against each of the other candidates. You can imagine a slow way to handle this: the voter considers each pair individually, choosing A vs B, A vs C, B vs C, etc.

To streamline this, the voter instead ranks the list of candidates. If, for example, the voter places A above B and B above C, they express their preference in all three head to head match ups at once. The ballot can be considered in a matrix like this:

ChoiceABC
A-WW
BL-W
CLL-

To calculate the full group of voters, we tally the total head-to-head wins from all the ballots. This gives us a matrix that might look something like this:

ChoiceABC
A-86
B2-7
C43-

A beats B (8 to 2) and A beats C (6 to 4), so A is the Condorcet winner.
