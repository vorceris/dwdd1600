Who Were the Signers

Famous Signers

Some of the most famous signers of the United States Declaration of Independence became major figures in early American history. Here are several of the best known:

John Hancock
Famous for his large, bold signature; as president of the Continental Congress, he signed first.
Thomas Jefferson
Main author of the Declaration of Independence and later the 3rd U.S. president.
Benjamin Franklin
One of the oldest signers, internationally famous as a scientist, diplomat, and writer.
John Adams
Strong advocate for independence and later the 2nd U.S. president.
Samuel Adams
Influential organizer of resistance to British rule in Massachusetts.
Roger Sherman
Only person to sign all four major founding U.S. documents.
Robert Morris
Helped finance the American Revolutionary War effort.
Charles Carroll
The only Catholic signer and the last surviving signer.
Richard Henry Lee
Introduced the resolution declaring the colonies independent.
Elbridge Gerry
Signer and later U.S. vice president; the term “gerrymandering” comes from his name.

A few interesting facts: There were 56 signers in total. Not all signed on July 4, 1776; most signed later that summer. Several signers later became presidents, governors, diplomats, or Supreme Court justices. Signing the document was considered treason against Britain and carried enormous personal risk.
